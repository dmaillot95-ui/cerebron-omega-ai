## Mission
Every newly mapped colony must learn the validated knowledge already acquired by SAPHEA MICRO and SAPHÉÏDE, follow new validated learning as it arrives, and actively question other colonies instead of passively copying their outputs.

## Bootstrap apprenticeship
Before specialist promotion, each colony receives:
1. CÉRÉBRON evidence rules and E0-E8 scale.
2. SAPHEA verified-training pipeline.
3. SAPHÉÏDE GOLD/SILVER/BRONZE/RED/OPEN knowledge classes.
4. BLIND DIVERGENCE -> INDEPENDENT CONVERGENCE -> OMEGA SIGNATURE protocol.
5. Provenance/dependency rules.
6. Failure/anti-error lessons.
7. Current validated AGORA lessons relevant to its domain.
8. Current OPEN questions, contradictions and unresolved blockers.

Imported knowledge is EDUCATION, not independent evidence.

## Continuous learning
At every validated learning event:
LESSON -> provenance check -> relevance routing -> colony inbox -> local comprehension task -> transfer exercise -> cold check -> local memory.

A colony must not claim it learned a lesson merely because the packet was delivered. Learning requires a successful novel transfer exercise.

## Completion criterion
A colony completes apprenticeship only after:
- consuming required validated lessons;
- passing comprehension checks;
- solving novel transfer tasks;
- asking or answering at least one useful AGORA question where relevant;
- passing audit and counter-audit;
- demonstrating no cold-benchmark leakage.
