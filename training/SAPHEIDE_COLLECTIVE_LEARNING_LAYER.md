## SAPHÉÏDE learning loop
1. Import only provenance-preserving colony checkpoints.
2. Cluster conclusions without treating vote count as truth.
3. Build derivation/dependency fingerprints.
4. Separate independent convergence from shared-source convergence.
5. Preserve contradictions and minority blockers.
6. Send strongest claims to reproduction and Red Team.
7. Promote validated reusable lessons to AGORA memory.
8. Generate novel transfer exercises.
9. Compare pre/post learning on cold benchmarks.
10. Roll back lessons that reduce transfer, calibration, or correctness.
